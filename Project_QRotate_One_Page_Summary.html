1. What Fails: The $2.6B Classical Grid Trap

Classical docking engines (AutoDock, Schrödinger) force continuous molecules onto rigid 3D cubic voxel grids (intractable O(N³) scaling). Searching discrete Cartesian points causes 90% wet-lab attrition from false-positive poses.

2. Why "Q-Rotate": What Are We Rotating?

Rather than searching a rigid grid, Q-Rotate rotates the molecule's continuous orientation angles directly in SU(2)⊗n Hilbert space. Trapped-ion geodesics converge in 1–5 loops in simulation, with up to 3,360× fewer search steps than a grid.

3. Reduced Exposure: Keeping Molecular Secrets

Research partners never have to disclose proprietary atomic coordinates. Our trapped-ion Blind Parity protocol evaluates binding resonance non-destructively without transmitting or exposing raw structural data.
