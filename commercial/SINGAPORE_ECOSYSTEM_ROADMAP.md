## 1. The Singapore Opportunity: Quantum Meets Biopolis

Singapore has positioned itself as the premier deep-tech and life sciences capital of Asia:
1. **The National Quantum Strategy (NQS):** Over **S$300 Million** committed by the National Research Foundation (NRF) to scale quantum hardware, algorithms, and talent.
2. **Helios Deployment:** Quantinuum’s next-generation **Helios trapped-ion quantum computer** arriving in Singapore represents the premier computational QPU in the region.
3. **Biopolis Biomedical Hub:** A concentrated, multi-billion-dollar life sciences cluster housing major global pharma R&D centers, A*STAR research institutes, and clinical trials.

Project Q-Rotate acts as the **software bridge** connecting Singapore's trapped-ion hardware infrastructure directly to Biopolis's biomedical pipelines.
